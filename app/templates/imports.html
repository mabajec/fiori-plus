How to export the right file from FIORI

The Spending by person and month view only fills in when the export includes the KadrŠt (personnel-number) column. That column is present only in the personnel-costs layout, so the view you pick in FIORI matters. To get it:

  1. Select the project you want to export.
  2. In the TOTAL row at the bottom, click Porabljeno to open the details.
  3. In Izbira izgleda, set the view to /OSEBJE_TS2 … Stroški osebja_TS.
  4. Click the Export icon (fourth from the right) and choose Lokalna datoteka.
  5. Pick Teksti s tabulatorji (tab-separated text) as the format.

Other layouts (without KadrŠt) still import and show correct totals, but the per-person grid will be empty because there's no personnel number to group by.
